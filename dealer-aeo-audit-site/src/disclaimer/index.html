Audit disclaimer.

No ranking or recommendation guarantee

No audit, website change, structured data implementation, content program, citation effort, protocol adoption, or gateway deployment can guarantee that an AI system will mention, cite, rank, or recommend a dealership.

Point-in-time observations

AI outputs can change between requests and may vary by model version, account, personalization, geography, source index, prompt wording, tool access, and platform availability. Dated observations describe what was found under the recorded conditions, not a universal result.

Evidence and inference

The audit distinguishes observed facts from analyst inference where practical. Technical findings should be validated in the dealer's production environment and with the responsible platform provider before implementation.

Not professional advice

Dealer AEO Audit is a marketing and technical diagnostic. It is not legal, regulatory, financial, accounting, security, accessibility, or compliance advice. Counsel and qualified specialists should review decisions in those areas.

Third-party systems

Dealer Growth Hackers does not control AI platforms, search engines, website providers, OEM systems, review sites, directories, or data feeds. Their behavior, availability, and policies may change without notice.

The honest promise: a disciplined evidence review, clear limitations, and an implementation plan tied to verifiable work—not guaranteed AI placement.
