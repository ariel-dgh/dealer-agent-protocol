Example Dealer Group

Market: Example City · Review date: September 5, 2026
38OUT OF 60

PARTIALLY VISIBLE

AI access is adequate. Entity clarity and third-party corroboration are not.

The site can be fetched, but important dealership facts are fragmented across markup, location pages, and external sources. This creates avoidable ambiguity in local recommendation answers.

FINDING
03.1
Observation
Rendered home-page markup identifies a LocalBusiness but not a complete AutoDealer entity.
Evidence
Brand relationships and area served are absent; the primary location identifier is inconsistent with the location page.
Impact
AI systems have weaker machine-readable evidence connecting the store, franchises, and served market.
Action
Publish one canonical AutoDealer entity and reference it consistently from the home and location pages.
Owner
Website platform provider, approved by dealership marketing.
Verify
Re-fetch rendered HTML, validate JSON-LD, compare identifiers across both pages, then rerun the original local query set.
PRIORITY
HIGH
Why now
The fix removes ambiguity affecting multiple query categories and can be completed without a new platform or data integration.
Dependency
Dealer confirms canonical business name, served markets, franchises, and location URL.
Acceptance
Valid markup is present in rendered HTML with no conflicting entity identifiers.

The difference is accountability

An automated tool can flag missing markup. The human-reviewed audit determines whether the flag is accurate, whether another source contradicts it, who controls the fix, and how the dealer will know the work was completed.
