Privacy policy.

This policy explains how Dealer Growth Hackers handles information submitted through DealerAEOAudit.com. Do not submit customer records, credentials, credit information, deal information, or other sensitive personal data.

Information we collect

An audit request may include your name, work email, organization, role, dealership website, market, rooftop count, website platform, audit trigger, optional visibility report link, stated audit goal, consent, source page, and ordinary security or access logs.

How we use it

We use submitted information to verify the requester and dealership, review the public website, define audit scope, respond about timing and commercial terms, prevent abuse, operate the service, and maintain required business records. We do not sell submitted personal information or publish an application.

Public website evidence

An audit may collect and retain publicly accessible pages, metadata, structured data, robots directives, discovery files, directory information, public reviews, cited sources, and dated AI answer observations. Reports may include screenshots or extracts needed to support a finding.

Analytics and providers

The site may use Google Analytics in a restricted consent configuration and honors Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals implemented by the browser. Hosting, analytics, database, communication, and audit records may be processed by contracted infrastructure providers acting for us.

Retention and security

Request and audit records are retained only as long as needed to fulfill the request, support follow-up work, meet legitimate business and legal needs, and protect the service. We use encrypted transport, access controls, validation, and data minimization, but no system is risk-free.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable by emailing ariel@dealergrowthhackers.com. We may retain records required by law or needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

This operating policy is not legal advice. Dealer Growth Hackers should have qualified counsel review production commercial terms and jurisdiction-specific obligations.
