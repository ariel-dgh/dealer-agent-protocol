Transparent by design

What the scan tests—and what it does not claim.

Dealer AI Visibility observes public website evidence. Every score must trace back to something reachable, readable, and reproducible. Missing or unavailable evidence is labeled honestly.

OBSERVED

A page, tag, file, rule, or response was actually retrieved.

INFERRED

A platform-readiness estimate is calculated from public signals and labeled as an estimate.

UNAVAILABLE

A failed fetch or provider test is never presented as a negative brand result.

Technical readiness / 75

Three pillars. Nineteen documented checks.

The raw technical score is the sum of three 25-point pillars and is rescaled to 100. Optional artifacts are reported without inflating the score.

01 / 25 POINTS

Schema markup

  • AutoDealer or LocalBusiness: 8
  • FAQPage: 5
  • Review or AggregateRating: 5
  • Product or Vehicle: 4
  • Any schema present: 3
02 / 25 POINTS

Page discoverability

  • Meta description: 3
  • Open Graph: 3
  • Canonical: 2
  • H1: 3; two H2s: 3
  • FAQ-style answers: 3
  • Freshness: 2; language: 2
  • Image alt coverage: 2
  • Five internal links: 2
03 / 25 POINTS

AI file readiness

  • Substantive llms.txt: 10
  • Minimal llms.txt: 5
  • robots.txt exists: 5
  • AI policy open: up to 10
  • Partial restrictions: 5
  • AI crawlers blocked: 0 policy points
  • Sitemap and AI manifest: reported, not scored
FORMULA(schema + page discoverability + AI files) ÷ 75 × 100

Grades: A 80–100 · B 65–79 · C 50–64 · D 35–49 · E 20–34 · F below 20

Visibility baseline

Six signal categories explain the commercial risk.

The scan also groups evidence into crawler access, structured meaning, citability, technical health, authority, and brand connection. Platform readiness estimates are derived from those public signals; they are not transcripts of live AI conversations.

What the free scan can establish

  • Whether key public files and page elements are reachable
  • Whether dealer, vehicle, FAQ, and review structure is visible in returned HTML
  • Whether content supplies direct, current, internally connected answers
  • Which technical gaps deserve attention first

What requires human verification

  • Dated buyer-intent queries across live answer engines
  • Accuracy, citations, competitor inclusion, and market context
  • JavaScript-rendered evidence and vendor-specific implementation constraints
  • Prioritization, ownership, remediation, and proof of correction

LIVE AI ANSWERS

A provider failure must display as Unavailable, never “Not mentioned.” Live results require the exact query, date, provider, response, and citations. Without that evidence, Dealer AI Visibility labels a number as a readiness estimate.
