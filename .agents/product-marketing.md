# Product marketing context

## Ecosystem

Dealer Growth Hackers operates a connected dealership AI retail stack:

- **Dealer AI Visibility** is the free, automated top-of-funnel signal check. It shows whether a dealership's public website is understandable to AI systems and produces a starting score.
- **Dealer AEO Audit** is the human-verified diagnostic and implementation plan. It validates evidence, explains why visibility or answer quality is weak, assigns fixes, and gives the dealer a vendor-ready action register.
- **DealershipMCP / Dealer Agent Gateway** is the implementation layer that makes current retail inventory, pricing, incentives, and disclosures available to agents in a governed format.
- **Dealer Query Dashboard** is the proof layer showing real agent queries, answers, citations, and operational health.
- **Dealer Agent Protocol** is the open standard that defines interoperable retail inventory and offer semantics.

## Dealer AEO Audit positioning

### Target customer

Dealer principals, general managers, marketing directors, and dealer-group digital leaders who know AI discovery matters but cannot tell whether a vendor report is accurate or what their website provider must change.

### Core problem

Automated scores identify symptoms but rarely provide defensible evidence, ownership, or a concrete implementation sequence. Dealers need a human-reviewed diagnosis that can be handed to internal teams and vendors.

### Value proposition

Dealer AEO Audit turns a dealership's AI visibility symptoms into a dated evidence packet and prioritized implementation plan: what failed, why it matters, who should fix it, and how the fix will be verified.

### Differentiators

- Automotive-specific 30-check framework across 10 dimensions.
- Human verification of high-impact findings instead of an unexplained score.
- Dealer- and market-specific query evidence where access permits.
- Vendor-ready action register with owner, evidence, priority, and verification criteria.
- Clear escalation path from diagnosis to implementation and measurement.

### Claims discipline

- Do not guarantee that an AI system will cite or recommend a dealership.
- Distinguish observed evidence from modeled or inferred findings.
- Date all answer-engine observations because outputs change.
- Do not use private pilot dealer names or data in public examples.
- Do not describe Dealer AEO Audit as another free AI visibility scanner.
